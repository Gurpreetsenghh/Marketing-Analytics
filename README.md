# 📊 E-Commerce Marketing Analytics

## 🚀 Project Summary

This project focuses on analyzing marketing and operational data from an e-commerce marketplace to uncover actionable business insights. By examining customer behavior, product performance, seller activity, and payment patterns, the analysis supports data-driven decision-making and business growth.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that help answer questions such as:

- How are sales performing over time?
- Which products and categories generate the highest revenue?
- How effectively are customers being retained?
- What buying patterns exist among customers?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- How satisfied are customers with their purchases?

---

## 📁 Dataset

The project combines multiple datasets, including:

- Orders & Transactions
- Customer Information
- Seller Information
- Product Details & Categories
- Payment Records
- Geographic Data

---

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

### 🧹 Data Preparation
- Cleaned missing and inconsistent values
- Combined multiple datasets into a unified analytical model
- Prepared data for analysis and visualization

### 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis
- Evaluated key business metrics
- Analyzed revenue, order volume, customer base, and seller activity
- Identified top-performing products and categories

### 📅 Trend Analysis
- Studied sales performance across:
  - Months
  - Weeks
  - Days
  - Hours
- Identified seasonal demand patterns

### 👥 Customer & Seller Segmentation
- Grouped customers based on purchasing behavior
- Analyzed seller performance and contribution

### 🛒 Market Basket Analysis
- Discovered products that are frequently purchased together
- Highlighted opportunities for cross-selling

### 💳 Payment Analysis
- Examined customer payment preferences
- Identified trends in payment timing and methods

### ⭐ Customer Feedback Analysis
- Evaluated product ratings and reviews
- Compared satisfaction levels across different product categories

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Sales exhibit noticeable seasonal fluctuations.
- Certain customer segments demonstrate stronger retention rates.
- Product combinations reveal valuable cross-selling opportunities.
- Regional differences influence payment preferences.
- Customer ratings help identify high-performing and underperforming product categories.
